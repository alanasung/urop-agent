Your ranked UROP shortlist

Every UROP on ELx, ranked for you. Prestige comes first: named sponsors and partners (GE Vernova, Lincoln Lab, NASA/JPL, US-government STTR, CERN, Brookhaven, the ISS) and marquee PIs. Then fit: AI, alignment and agents at the top, hardware welcome. Remote postings get a small bump.

How the score works: prestige × 10 + fit × 4 + 2 if remote. Prestige 3 means a named external sponsor or partner, or a Nobel-laureate PI. Prestige 2 means a marquee lab or PI, or a Mass General collaboration. Fit 5 means core AI (agents, RL, alignment or interpretability). Fit 3 means hardware, security or systems work.
MISTI and study-abroad programs are left out. Postings whose own application deadline has passed are hidden unless you turn on “Show closed”. Pay listed as “TBD” usually means UROP direct funding, and that deadline has already passed for Fall.
